# WPA Repair Tags (trial)

World Pro Audio's in-house repair tag app.

- `index.html`: the counter app. Enter the job on the orange tag form, create the tag, text or email the customer their stub, print the tag and stub, and look up past tags.
- `stub.html`: the customer's digital claim stub, opened from the link they're sent.
- Installs to a phone or iPad home screen (`manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`) and works offline.

Trial limits: texts and emails open the device's own Messages or Mail app, and tags are saved on the device that made them.
